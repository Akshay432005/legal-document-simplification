Quick Instructions

Our NLP engine performs the following processing steps to simplify your legal documents:

  • Extract Text: Pulls plain text from formatting structures (PDF tables, docx XML elements, image OCR).
  • Legal Term Detection: Scans for archaic jargon (e.g., indemnify, herein, mutatis mutandis) and maps them to plain synonyms.
  • Sentence Segmentation: Splits verbose sentences containing multiple nested structures.
  • Readability Scoring: Computes immediate reading ease before & after metrics using the Flesch index.
  • Verification Check: Uses word overlaps and vectors to check meaning preservation.
OCR Tip: Scan documents with high lighting and low shadows. Standard contracts will execute automatically.
